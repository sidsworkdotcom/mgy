Frequently asked

Before your first meeting

What should I bring to a consultation?

Bring any documents connected to your matter, such as agreements, notices, court papers, correspondence and identity documents, along with a short timeline of events. This helps us assess where you stand from the first meeting.

Do you take matters outside Nagpur?

Yes. The practice is based in Nagpur, and matters in other cities and forums are taken up depending on the jurisdiction and the nature of the proceedings.

Can I consult online?

Yes. Online consultations are available by appointment, depending on what the matter requires.

Is what I share kept confidential?

Yes. Client information is handled with professional confidentiality and communication is kept to appropriate professional channels.
