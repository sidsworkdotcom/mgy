Practice areas

How we can help

The firm combines courtroom advocacy with careful research and drafting. Each matter begins with understanding the client's objective, followed by advice that is legally sound and commercially practical.

Civil and commercial litigation

Representation in civil and commercial disputes, including suits, recovery, injunctions, contractual disputes and appeals, supported by detailed pleadings and legal research.

Corporate and business advisory

Practical legal support for businesses, entrepreneurs and startups, including agreements, compliance, commercial transactions and dispute prevention.

Arbitration and dispute resolution

Handling of domestic and international arbitration and other dispute-resolution matters, with a focus on efficient and commercially workable outcomes.

Family and matrimonial

Confidential and considered assistance in sensitive family disputes, including divorce, maintenance, custody and succession.

Intellectual property

Advice and representation on the protection, registration and enforcement of trademarks, copyright and other intellectual-property rights.

Property and land

Title checks, property disputes, partition matters and documentation for property transactions.

Criminal law

Advice and representation in criminal proceedings, including complaints, bail and trial matters.

Consumer disputes

Complaints and representation before consumer commissions.

Employment and labour

Advice on employment contracts, workplace issues and labour-law disputes for employers and employees.
